# Mission: Graph Neural Networks

## Why
Build a deep conceptual understanding of how Graph Neural Networks work, coming from a strong ML background. The goal is to understand the key ideas, architectures, and design choices behind GNNs well enough to read papers, evaluate when GNNs are the right tool, and potentially apply them to future projects.

## Success looks like
- Can explain the message-passing framework and why it works on graph-structured data
- Can distinguish between GCN, GAT, GraphSAGE, and other major variants and articulate their trade-offs
- Can read a GNN paper and follow the architecture and training procedure
- Understands what tasks GNNs solve (node classification, link prediction, graph classification) and how the architecture adapts to each

## Constraints
- Advanced ML background (CNNs, RNNs, backpropagation, optimisation) — no need to re-teach neural network basics
- Curiosity-driven learning — no immediate deadline or project pressure

## Out of scope
- Production deployment and scaling of GNNs (for now)
- Exhaustive survey of every GNN variant
- Deep graph theory proofs
